Fonts for slides. They are offered in the slide editor and used when slides are rendered; restart the app after
changing them. See INSTALLATION.md (Slides).

- .ttf or .otf files: offered under the names stored in the files.
- google-fonts.txt: the embed code from fonts.google.com (the @import or <link> lines). The fonts are downloaded at
  startup and kept in the storage volume.
